# Image credits

All 44 photographs and paintings in `images/` are **public-domain** works
obtained from Wikimedia Commons and downloaded into this repository so the site
works offline and does not hotlink Wikimedia's servers. Each was resized to a
maximum of 900px and re-encoded as JPEG.

Four stations deliberately carry no photograph and keep their drawn vignette
instead: the Bengal famines of 1770 and 1943, the 1853 railway, and the 1670 night assault on Sinhagad (no public-domain period image exists). For the
famines no suitable non-graphic period image was found, and a bare drawn bowl is
a more respectful choice than a photograph of the dead.
